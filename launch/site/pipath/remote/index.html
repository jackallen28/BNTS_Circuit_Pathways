Control your Raspberry Pi from the browser

1. Link to your Pi

Turn your Raspberry Pi on and wait a minute. Pi Link starts by itself. Then type the Pi ID from the label on your Pi, like K7M2-QX9P.

Before you wire: turn the Pi's HAT off the pins and unplug wires before changing them. Use a 330Ω resistor with every LED. Pins give 3.3V only: never connect 5V to a GPIO pin.

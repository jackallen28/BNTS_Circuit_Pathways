1. My classes

Make a class and give students its code. They type it on the home page with their first name(s) and school: that is all that is kept about them, with their work, on this school's own Circuit Pathways server (nothing goes to the internet). Set the work you want them to do, and follow their progress here. When the class ends, the class, the names and the work are deleted.

    2. Class files (optional)

    A class file (.circuitclass) is saved on your computer. It holds a class's name and the students' work, so you can keep it after the class ends, or look at work students saved as files on a computer that wasn't on the school network.

    Student work

    Students save their own work with Settings > Save my work and give you their .circuit (ArduinoPath) or .pipath (PiPath) files. Add them here to see who has done what.
